Find the scenario, answer the question in the first column, then follow the action. Always acknowledge the customer first, then work the case. To escalate to the store manager (KMP), send the Helpscout link to the KMP channel of the CG CSR team with the context. SP = Service Point, HS = Helpscout, WFTU = waiting for tracking update, CS Sheet = Customer Support Sheet, RTS = return to sender, COGS = our cost for the item.

 Acknowledge first. Politely ask why they want to cancel.

 Present both options and ask which they prefer. The 10% is our thank-you for letting the order go ahead.
Customer accepts the 10% discount
Order proceeds

  • Tell the team in the Slack thread to release the hold so the order ships

 Acknowledge and hold while the store manager replies.

Monitoring: every cancellation request goes into the Customer Support Sheet, under Cancellation monitoring, as soon as it comes in. The sheet should have all of them, even when the order proceeds or the customer only wants a variant change.

Variant or item changeCustomer wants to cancel in order to change the color, variant, or item

QuestionInternal actionTell the customer
Customer wants to cancel to change variant (e.g. color) or item
  • The customer does not need to cancel. We do the exchange ourselves
  • Duplicate the existing order in Shopify
  • In the Draft order that gets generated, replace the variant (color change) or the product (item change)
  • Edit the price in Shopify so it is 0 if it is the same product or value
  • Reason: exchange or change color
  • CANCEL the original order in SP and in Shopify
 Acknowledge first. Say we will take care of the exchange so they do not need to cancel.
YESIs there a price difference? Send an invoice for the difference via Shopify. Explain the difference and that an invoice will be sent via Shopify.
NOSame product or value? Keep the Draft order price at 0. No invoice. Confirm the change and the timeline.

Why we do this: it is easier for the customer. They do not have to cancel and reorder, so we exchange it for them.

RTS: return to senderThe parcel is on its way back to the sender

ScenarioInternal actionTell the customer
Address issue, as per the local courier
  • Check immediately in the supplier's Slack channel if they can get a document from the local courier showing the address is wrong
  • Ask the supplier if a redelivery is possible
  • If the customer says the address is correct: ask if they are OK with a redelivery, and ask for the detailed address and mobile number again
  • If reshipment is no longer possible: we usually pay 7 EUR as a custom order (redelivery fee)
 If the customer emailed: acknowledge the email first. Ask for the complete address, and ask if anyone has messaged them. Say that, as per our check, there is an address issue, and we are checking with dispatch.
Rejected by the customer
  • Use the customer support template for this
  • Collect a 10% restocking fee
  • We cannot get the item back to resend it
 Explain that the customer needs to collect the item and go through the return process to get a refund. A parcel returned to the sender is not sent back to us. It gets destroyed at the international warehouse. Mention the 10% restocking fee.
RTS due to size
No delivery attempt was made to the customer
  • Verify with the supplier if there is any way to ship it
  • If there is no way to ship it, offer other products to the customer
 Acknowledge first. Explain it could not be delivered and offer the alternatives.
Remote address
  • Usually visible in the supplier's Slack channel or in SP
  • Proceed with a full refund. This is rare
 Explain the address is remote and cannot be delivered. Confirm the full refund and the timeline.
